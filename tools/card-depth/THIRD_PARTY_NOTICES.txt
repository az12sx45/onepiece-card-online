The model weights remain in D:/Codex_Tools/card-depth-sam-v1/models, outside Git/public.
sam_refine.py adapts the MIT rembg ORT interface/preprocessing, with changes:
OpenCV affine interpolation, separate encoder/decoder lifetimes, CPU/RAM limits,
candidate selection rather than union, fixed pilot prompts, and provenance/QA output.
Later local changes add explicit per-card JSON jobs, crop/rotation coordinate mapping,
source/model-bound embedding NPZ caches, and cache-only decoder mode.
No SAM model inference happens in the website and no card image is uploaded.
