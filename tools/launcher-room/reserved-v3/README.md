# Ten locked One Piece room characters — art handoff

This directory is **work in progress**, separate from the four `reserved_v1` characters. The ten IDs in `roster.json` are canon One Piece characters and are not released, sold, granted, or usable. A finished portrait or turnaround does not imply a finished motion pack. The server release flags currently cover only Ace, Sabo, Law and Hancock.

## Source of truth and visual rules

`roster.json` records selected source PNGs and their SHA-256, official character pages, costume periods, and remaining source sheets. These are built-in GPT image generations reviewed by a model, not human art acceptance. The first turnaround is stored under `sources/<key>/master-01/source.png`; selected C-drive generated copies were removed only after the D-drive hash matched. Some source previews showed gray RGB in pixels with alpha zero. The alpha channel, rather than the viewer's RGB preview, determines whether a background is present. Failed Vivi sheets with figures crossing cell boundaries were rejected and their unselected PNGs removed.

Use the existing `reserved-v1` source contract (`CANON_AND_ART_SPEC.md`, `import_atlases.py`) as the target. Each character needs **seven distinct accepted source sheets**: `master` (2×2 east/west/north/south), `walk-side` (3×2 east/west contact A–neutral–contact C), `walk-front` (3×2 north/south), `acting-side` (4×4 eight poses east and west), `acting-front` (4×4 north and south), `work` (4×4 four frames in each direction), and `utility` (4×4 eat/rest/sleep/train, four frames each). All figures must be whole and connected, the head and torso must face the same direction, and walking steps must remain low. Use the completed Ace/Sabo/Law/Hancock sheets to calibrate standing height. Preserve each canonical costume and asymmetric feature in every view. Do not mirror the figure or assemble body parts.

Every source sheet must have genuine alpha, including cell gutters. Reject sheets with opaque scenery or haze, shadows, separated anatomy, extra hands, inconsistent outfit, oversized heads, or repeated same-foot walk contacts. `grid_qa.py` finds transparent source cut lines and records them in `roster.json` for selected sheets. The original image is not redrawn; `export_character.py` crops whole figures from those recorded regions and uses uniform whole-image scaling. Inspect every cell at native size. Do not generate synthetic placeholder animation and call it accepted art.

The target runtime contract per character is **17 WebP files** under `public/images/launcher_room/reserved_v3/<key>/`: `portrait.webp` (256×256), `walk/{east,west,north,south}.webp` (1536×384, 4 frames of 384), `acting/{east,west,north,south}.webp` (2048×256, 8 frames of 256), `life/work-{east,west,north,south}.webp` (1024×256, 4 frames of 256), and `life/{eat,rest,sleep,train}-south.webp` (1024×256, 4 frames of 256). The logical root is (64,112) in a 128-pixel game cell. The existing importer can be adapted to run one character at a time, but only the exact selected GPT originals may supply body drawings. Exporting PNGs into WebP is not a substitute for examining the final 81-frame contact sheet for that character.

Run `python tools/launcher-room/reserved-v3/verify_art.py --report <new-report.json>` after each handoff. A character becomes art-ready only when all 7 sources and 17 outputs pass this verifier **and** the actual 81-frame contact sheet is reviewed for anatomy, action, identity and proportion. Do not flip a release flag merely because a master exists.

The complete Vivi candidate has 7 selected source sheets and 17 lossless WebP atlases with 81 frames, 3,473,672 runtime asset bytes, an actual contact sheet, `manifests/vivi.json`, and `pixel-qa/vivi.json`. It remains locked and is **not in the 1.2.15 installer**; its contact review is model-only. The other nine do not have complete 7-sheet/17-asset packs. All ten have small transparent previews under `public/images/launcher_room/reserved_v3_previews/`, with independent hashes in `preview-manifest.json`. The installer is near its 256 MiB cap. Distribute later completed locked packs via an independently hashed download asset pack; downloading must not grant ownership or bypass the server release gate.

## Relationship notes for future dialogue

The room is a cross-period collection base, not a claim that all characters lived together in canon. Vivi is a trusted former travelling companion of the Straw Hats; Shanks is Luffy's admired pirate and has lost his left arm; Mihawk trained Zoro and has a separate rivalry with Shanks; Perona grudgingly aided Zoro; Marco and Ace share Whitebeard family ties; Buggy and Shanks knew each other as Roger apprentices; Carrot travelled with the crew but is a Mink of Zou; Yamato is connected to Ace and admires Oden but is not a Straw Hat member; Bentham repeatedly sacrificed for Luffy's friendship; Koala is Sabo's fellow Revolutionary Army member and has a history with the Sun Pirates. New room lines must be original, natural daily-life exchanges consistent with these relationships, without inventing a canon event or turning every sentence into a catchphrase.
