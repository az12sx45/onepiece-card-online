## Drawing and export

Each selected character has seven GPT source sheets: master, walk-side, walk-front, acting-side, acting-front, work and utility. Preserve every original generation, prompt and receipt; selected-source SHA and exact regions are recorded by the importer. Earlier retries remain provenance and are not packaged as player assets.

Use complete connected figures, matching head/body direction, small low-foot walking steps, and distinct A/neutral/C/neutral contact frames. No detached heads, limb assembly, mirrored direction, procedural anatomy or per-pose stretching. Export uses whole-figure alpha extraction, a shared standing calibration per source, uniform scaling and translation. Transparent RGB and distant low-alpha haze are discarded by the established connected-component alpha processing, without repainting the character.

Each character publishes 17 WebP files: portrait 256x256; four walk atlases 1536x384; four acting atlases 2048x256; four work and eat/rest/sleep/train atlases 1024x256. Logical cell is 128, root (64,112). New life clips have 256px native cells; original ten characters keep their existing paths and stride.

Source-pixel validation and model visual review are distinct. Synthetic importer fixtures are never accepted as generated art. Final review must bind the actual exported contact sheets by SHA and report its non-human-review limitation.
